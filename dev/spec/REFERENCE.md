## Look & feel common to all maps (Unreal Engine 5, realistic PBR)
- Photoreal materials: heavy texture detail, normal maps, glossy reflective floors (polished tile shows reflections),
  soft GI, strong AO in corners. Props are dense and cluttered, at realistic scale.
- Some props are deliberately oversized or toy-like (giant plush penguins, big balloons, giant octopus, pufferfish
  lantern). Cut-out standees (cows) stand on wooden A-frame feet.
- Player: plain white rounded mannequin (no face), about 1.7 m.
