# MECCHA CHAMELEON (めっちゃカメレオン, Steam 4704690) — consolidated research

Sources: WebSearch summaries (page fetches blocked). Reliable: JP press (4Gamer, AUTOMATON, Game*Spark, Famitsu,
Denfaminicogamer, ASCII, Dengeki), GameWith, GameRant, DualShockers, dev X posts. Many English "wiki" domains are
SEO/AI-generated (lower confidence). UNCERTAIN = not confirmed.

## General
- Devs: Lemorion_1224 + Haganeiro. Steam 2026-06-10, Switch 2 2026-09-09. Unity-style realistic-ish 3D interiors, white
  rounded humanoid "chameleons" that paint themselves to blend in. Hunters carry a paint SHOTGUN (pump, HL2-like).
- Style note: maps are fairly realistic PBR interiors full of props (that's why painting to blend in works). Many
  flat single-colour surfaces in Indoor Country; realistic textures in Mansion/Sewer/Backrooms.
- **Random prop placement** per round exists on some maps (Mansion since ~v2.x, Penguin Hotel 2.7.0, Indoor Country).

### Hide-and-Seek Mansion
- LARGEST map, best for beginners, classic European mansion, multi-room. Default map.
- Areas (GameRant): busy intricate main hall/lobby; large industrial kitchen; full library; laundry room; storage areas.
  Fan sites add: tiled bathroom, side rooms, hallways with gilded-frame paintings, maybe a ballroom (UNCERTAIN).
- Main hall: tall space with stone columns/pillars (climbable, right at spawn) and ceiling beams; large dark-metallic
  HORSE STATUE; red armchairs.
- Library: rows of bookshelves with colour-blocked book spines. Kitchen: cabinets, shelves, lots of clutter, wall posters.
- Bathroom: uniform two-colour alternating tiles on walls AND floor. Laundry: chaotic clutter, BIRD STATUE on a shelf.
- Storage room (far back): boxes, barrels, pallets, BRICK WALL. Hallway paintings with gold frames, warm wood + gold.
  A side room with a black tiled wall; wooden arch walls. GameWith: bookshelves, paintings, pillars, tiles, furniture.
- Second floor: not confirmed. Lighting: warm indoor (UNCERTAIN).

### Indoor Country
- Indoor farm that looks like a MOVIE SET: flat plywood cut-outs (standees) mixed with full-size props. SMALL map with many
  objects. Cow & chicken standee signboards, vegetables, hay bales, red barn doors, pumpkins, cartoon SKY MURALS on walls,
  fences, wood, hanging cloud decorations. Bright, warm, many flat single-colour surfaces. High beams/rafters to climb.

### Sewer
- Stagnant, grimy, DARK. Drum cans (oil drums), sandbags, GRAFFITI everywhere (two graffiti sections), pipes, brick,
  barrels. Big round rusty pipes, poured concrete, concrete pillars, flowing water channels, corrugated metal panels,
  tiled walls, metal grate floors, dim flickering sparse lights. Layout = walkways along the sewer pipes, not rooms.

### Backrooms
- Yellow walls, repeating corridors, liminal office maze, worn (yellow-brown) carpet, bright even fluorescent ceiling
  lights, few shadows. Each area has ONE kind of object grouped together: many TOILETS, many ROAD SIGNS; also bikes
  mounted on walls, stacks of plastic chairs, red glowing EXIT signs, vending machines. Small and fairly empty.

### Penguin Hotel
- Developer's own "Penguin Hotel" series. Medium size, BLUE theme, lots of penguin PLUSHIES and BALLOONS, GIANT PENGUIN
  STATUE in the centre. Busy central ballroom, second-floor overlook (mezzanine), side bedrooms, narrow hallways,
  lobby tiles, bathrooms with glossy tiles + bathtubs, beds + pillows, ceramic penguin statues, rubber ducks.

### Sugarland
- Land of sweets: mountains made of candy, candies stuck in the ground, many GINGERBREAD cookie figures, bright cute
  fairy-tale mood. Gumdrops, gingerbread houses (stand on/hide between), chocolate hills, white cake with strawberries,
  a tree, candy rocks, "locker corner" (UNCERTAIN). Large, saturated.

### Osaka
- SMALL shopping-street (商店街) town that looks a bit like a wasteland; Dotonbori feel. Osaka-themed shop signs incl.
  gyoza shop and a giant SEA-URCHIN sign (parody of the crab sign), rolling TUMBLEWEEDS, drums, signs, garbage bags,
  pipes, frames, narrow paths, cluttered nooks.

### Egypt
- Mysterious ruins: ancient-Egyptian buildings, hieroglyph writing, a throne, grave goods; pillars, corners, stairs,
  entrances. (pyramid/sphinx not confirmed)
### Death Burger
- Burger-shop-themed eerie amusement park, American-horror-game feel: hamburger monsters, clown-like character, burger
  with a giant eyeball; front counter, kitchen line, dining booths, menu boards. Smallish.
### Greece
- Santorini style: white buildings + blue domes/shutters, simple blue-and-white palette, SUNSET light; outdoor stair
  lanes, plaza, main street with café shutters. Designed for glow paint.
### Deep Sea
- Colourful tropical fish, jellyfish, seaweed, shipwreck debris, coral, giant clams, a UNICORN STATUE.
### Shopping Mall
- Sales floors full of goods piled on the ground and on shelves; not large; easy to blend in.
### Kyoto
- Based on Kyoto Station: large TRUSS-structure ceiling, GRAND STAIRCASE, lanterns (chochin), torii gates, Japanese
  umbrellas, dolls in traditional clothes, autumn maple motifs, lantern light — like a night event at the station.
### Art Museum
- Paintings from around the world, objects from the game itself, miniatures of famous tourist spots.

## Lobby (開始待機ロビー)
- 3D room you walk around in. A CANVAS with "PLAY" written on it: stand in front, press E → rule settings + start
  (host only). Older versions: a light-blue button at the edge.
- A red HUNTER platform/pad in the MIDDLE of the lobby: standing on it at start gives priority to be hunter (still limited
  by hunter count). Otherwise random.
- HUNTERS WAIT IN THE LOBBY during the hide phase (can't see the map); they can paint themselves (F) and RMB for TPS.
- Host: settings canvas → pick map, mode, rules, filters → START (はじめる) bottom-left of the settings screen →
  countdown (~15 s, UNCERTAIN) during which a box pops up: size choices at top, Cube character at bottom; current map
  can be checked before start.
- Players wait: walk around, chat, paint themselves. (Map preview boards: NOT confirmed — user remembers maps shown.)

## Settings (host)
- Game Mode: Basic / Increasing Oni (infection) / Double / Reverse Chicken Race.
- Filters (combinable): Monochrome, Horror, Mosaic — middle column of the Configure-Map screen.
- Map: official maps list; Random map with per-map include toggle (collab maps off by default).
- Number of Hunters; Hunter Wait Time (= hide time); Search Time; Answer Check Time; Forced Taunt (whistle) interval
  (min 5 s; manual whistle resets it); Show missed-points ranking to hunters (on/off); Ammo Limit (on/off, 1–99, default
  5; miss −1, hit +1, shots at fleeing chameleon free, all hunters at 0 → hiders win); Allow Petit Chameleon; Hunter TPS.
- Defaults for times/hunters: UNCERTAIN. Community values: hide 75–200 s, search 200–300 s, answer check 45–60 s,
  taunt 30–45 s.
- Vote skip "もうええよ" (everyone votes → skip hide time / answer check). BAN function.
- Create Server asks: display name, server name, password (private), max players. 4.1.0: 5-character join codes.
- Options (Esc): motion blur, fps cap, shadow quality, resolution/window, performance mode; sensitivity, volume, language,
  keybinds (labels UNCERTAIN).

## Modes
- Basic: fixed teams; hunters must hit every chameleon in time, one survivor → hiders win. Found hiders spectate.
- Increasing Oni: hit chameleon joins hunters.
- Double: everyone hides, then everyone hunts on the same field; whoever finds everyone fastest / most wins.
- Reverse Chicken Race (2.4.0): hiders choose paint + pose, these are SHOWN to the hunters; short search; first hunter to
  find a given hider scores; hiders earn missed points while in view but not shot; single top scorer wins.
- Missed points (見逃し/見落としポイント): hiders gain points in real time while in a hunter's view and not caught; clones
  count; shooting a clone eliminates its owner.
- Answer check (答え合わせ): all hiding spots revealed; "ええやん" (like) button.

## HUD
- Top: timer + target counter; row of hider icons white → red when found. Red magnifying-glass icon = hunter's find
  count/score. Green diamonds at the bottom = CLONE COOLDOWN. Key guide on the RIGHT side. No hide-HUD option.
- Timer format/font/colours: UNCERTAIN.

## Character
- Pure-white rounded ("blobby") humanoid silhouette, small, two legs; face/joints not described (likely featureless).
- Body types: Default (rounded) / Cube (1.9.0) / Plump ふくよか (3.3.0). Size: Petit Chameleon ×0.5 (4.0.0, host must
  allow). No other sizes.
- Clones: max 2 (Q create, X remove all); same appearance; cooldown shown by green diamonds.
- Poses: ~35 total. Launch 8: stand ちょくりつ, sit おすわり, lie ねころび, spread-eagle だいのじ, lump かたまり,
  cactus さぼてん, surrender こうさん (+1). 1.8.0 (+11): lying on side, cross-legged, bridge, one hand raised, forward
  bend, T-pose, hands on hips, … 3.2.0 (+12): handstand, both hands raised, homage poses. Also: crouch, zazen, airplane
  arms ("ブーン"). Names are NOT shown in game.
- Pose wheel: hold R, move mouse toward a pose (highlights BLUE), release R to confirm; click the centre to change page.

## Controls (PC)
WASD move · Shift sprint · Space jump / climb (jump into a wall/object and hold Space to climb; can travel along walls and
ceilings) · Ctrl crouch / climb down · F paint mode · hold Space in paint mode = 3D eyedropper (samples real colour +
surface properties) · LMB paint · hold RMB drag = brush size · MMB drag / Alt+LMB = rotate paint camera · wheel / Alt+RMB
zoom · wheel = switch saved colours · hold R pose wheel · Q clone · X remove clones · 1 whistle · 2 nameplates ·
3 see-through · 5 free camera · 4 free-camera free movement · G like · B mute players · T chat · Esc settings ·
E interact · Hunter: LMB shoot, RMB FPS/TPS (if host enabled). Rotate in place + rotation lock exist (keys UNCERTAIN).

## Hunter / shooting
- Paint SHOTGUN, first-person by default (TPS optional). Shells leave PAINT TRAILS and splash paint on impact; each shot
  "splatters RAINBOW colours around the room"; the MUZZLE FLASH lights up the area. Shot delay 1.5 s. Hit = found.

## Painting
- Colour palette top-left; 3D eyedropper (Space); several picked colours kept, wheel switches; Metallic slider (right =
  shiny metal); Roughness (right = matte); Emissive (2.9.0); shadow toggle in paint mode; brush size via RMB drag.
  No confirmed hex/alpha/undo.
